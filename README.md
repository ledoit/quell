# Quell

One window. It opens on the OLL pattern grid. OLL / PLL swaps that grid for the perm letters. A pattern opens that pattern’s cases. A case opens that alg, alone, in the center.

Soft and Hard are the two looks. Faces sits beside them. Names is not in the product.

Tap outside the alg to come back. On Hard, Close does the same. The name in the corner comes back to the grid.

Algorithms are the SpeedCubeDB 3x3 standard, in their notation, with cube rotations written out so the case matches the moves. Every case shows its side stickers. An OLL’s other colors are one of the arrangements that keep its yellow pattern. Faces draws a legal color grip, and the OLL top is then the real stickers. A case can be shown as its standard algorithm, a turn of the top face, or the left-hand mirror of an algorithm already in the set. Quiz drills one case at a time, three algorithms stacked. The other choices are the closest algorithms. A right tap turns green and the next case follows. A wrong tap turns red, the right one turns green, and the next case waits for a tap.
