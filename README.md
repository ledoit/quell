# Quell

One window. It opens on the OLL pattern grid. OLL / PLL swaps that grid for the perm letters. A pattern opens that pattern’s cases. A case opens that alg, alone, in the center.

Soft and Hard are the two looks. The menu switches them. Names is not in the product.

Tap outside the alg to come back. On Hard, Close does the same. The name in the corner comes back to the grid.

Algorithms are the SpeedCubeDB 3x3 standard, in their notation, with cube rotations written out so the case matches the moves. Every case shows its side stickers. An OLL’s other colors are one of the arrangements that keep its yellow pattern. Quiz drills one case at a time, three algorithms stacked. The other choices are the closest algorithms.
